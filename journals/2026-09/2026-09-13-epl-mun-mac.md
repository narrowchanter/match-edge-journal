## Why this caused the learning (link event → rule)

1. **Red alone** does not justify buying the 11 at ~55¢ when the 10 were pre-match favourites with Haaland still on. Greenberg PL: home only gains ~**+0.36 pts/game** when *away* goes to 10 (1.69→2.05). Equal-strength red~30' → 11-win ~62% / 10-win ~18% — United @52–56 was ~that band *without* quality-adjusting down for City.
2. **Live cause:** United had the man and **still produced HT xG ~0.16**, posts without control, slow circulation, City clean sheet + composure. That is **man advantage without pitch control**.
3. **Ash call:** once that tape is visible (~15–25' after red / HT), the process side is **depressed City Yes (~teens) or Draw**, not forced United Yes. Immediate post-red City ~18¢ ≈ fair on equal-strength models; **edge appears after the 11 fails to convert**.
4. FT City win is the tail (~15–24% for 10-man wins historically) — rude, but the *class of failure* (low-block + elite 9 eating chalk) was readable from HT xG.
