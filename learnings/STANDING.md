# Standing rules

Only rules with a dated journal citation. Detail lives in the journal — this file is the index.

## Exits — chop → first goal

**Rule:** On favorite Yes after a 0–0 chop then first goal, **bank 50–100%** into the spike (lean **full** when away / live xG flat / chaos). Trail a runner only if minute/venue historical convert ≥ mark **and** live control (xG lead, not parity). Do not rewrite a good sell with FT.

**Why / source:** [2026-09-12 Sunderland–Arsenal](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-12-epl-sun-ars.md) — Ash full sell ARS Yes 56→80¢ ~62' after Guimarães 58'; even xG + Le Fée missed pen; away +1 historically &lt;~70% convert until ~68' (Sudol PL). FT 0–2 is outcome noise. **Reinforce 2026-09-20:** [Fulham–Man United](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-20-epl-ful-mun.md) — Martínez OG 63' marked FUL Yes ~79.5¢; no SELL; Cunha 89' → FT 1–1; Gina −$5.19.

## Exits — chalk already 2-up ≥70¢ → bank; multi-goal collapse → full kill

**Rule:** On **chalk Yes already 2-up** with mark **≥70¢** (especially **≥90¢**), **bank 50–100%** into that spike. If a later **full multi-goal collapse** hits (e.g. 2–0 → 2–2), **full exit** — do **not** hold for a stoppage winner. FT lottery hits do not validate the hold. Extends chop→first-goal bank to multi-goal chalk leads. **Also applies to Draw holdings:** when Gina holds Draw and the board shows favorite/away chalk **≥70¢** on a multi-goal lead path, bank/kill the Draw — don’t wait for stoppage or an OG bounce.

**Why / source:** [2026-09-15 Elche–Real Madrid](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-15-lal-elc-rea.md) — Gina RMA Yes 6.10sh @82¢; 0–2 marked ~97–98¢ with no SELL; Osorio 71' / Niño 83' → 2–2 kill missed; Espí 90+1' paid ≈+$1.10 — process fail, FT noise. **Draw extension 2026-09-20:** [Valencia–Real Sociedad](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-20-lal-val-rso.md) — Draw held through Soler pen 1–2 / RSO ~77¢ exit alert; Beitia OG 2–2 then Barrenetxea 90'+1 → FT 2–3; ≈−$5.

## Exits — auto-bank when Gina holds (alert ≠ trade)

**Rule:** When Gina **holds** a live soccer ticket and chop→first-goal or chalk ≥70¢ (incl. Draw extension above) opens on **our** mark, **execute bank SELL 50–100%** (lean full when away / xG flat / chaos). Do **not** leave the bank window as analysis-only / pm-hook text. Standing auto-probe entries without matching auto-banks is the asymmetry to close. Opponent first-goal spikes still do **not** trigger bank on our Yes (sit scrap / don’t morph).

**Why / source:** [2026-09-20 Fulham–Man United](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-20-epl-ful-mun.md) — auto-probe FUL Yes @23¢; Martínez OG bank ~79.5¢ alerted, **sellVolume $0**; Cunha wipe. Sister same night: [Valencia Draw exit skipped](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-20-lal-val-rso.md). Contrast [Deportivo–Betis](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-20-lal-dep-bet.md) — Cucho concede correctly did **not** fire bank.

## Live — post-red (away early red)

**Rule:** Do **not** auto-buy the 11 after an away early red. Quality-adjust (Greenberg PL: home only ~+0.36 pts/game when away goes to 10). If the 11 fails to generate xG/territory for ~15–25' or by HT, prefer **depressed 10-man Yes or Draw** over forced 11-Yes. Immediate post-red 10-Yes ~fair on equal-strength models; edge appears after the 11’s dead tape.

**Why / source:** [2026-09-13 Man United–Man City](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-13-epl-mun-mac.md) — Foden red ~23'; United Yes ~52–56 trap; HT United xG ~0.16; Ash sat; leave-on-table was City Yes ~teens once tape was dead; City 1–0 Haaland with 10 men. **Reinforce:** [2026-09-18 Espanyol–Elche](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-18-lal-esp-elc.md) — Redondo (Elche) red 6'; Espanyol = 11 still lost 1–3. **Paper 2026-09-20:** Atlético beat 10-man Real Madrid 2–1 after Huijsen red 52' ([sunday paper](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-20-sunday-paper.md)) — ATM Yes ≤28 leave-on-table if unfilled; still not an auto-buy-the-11 license.

## Live — missed Draw window → don’t morph to late +0.5 / favorite-No

**Rule:** If the desk already said **don’t chase** Draw (price left the edge band, e.g. mid-20s → ~40¢+), **PASS**. Do **not** morph the same late 0–0 narrative into **home +0.5 / favorite No @ ~65–75¢**. Same story, worse instrument, still fully dead to one late pen/set-piece favorite win. Tiny size doesn’t fix a no-edge entry.

**Why / source:** [2026-09-13 Real Sociedad–Atlético Madrid](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-13-lal-rso-mad.md) — morning Draw ~26.5¢ was the edge; live ~58' Draw ~42.5 tagged don’t-chase; Ash $5 Atlético No @~70¢; FT 0–3. **Held 2026-09-20:** Fulham post-Cunha Draw @~92¢ refused; Valencia RSO Yes @77 refused ([ful-mun](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-20-epl-ful-mun.md), [val-rso](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-20-lal-val-rso.md)).

## Pre-match — MED–HIGH clearest board → probe by default

**Rule:** When the morning slate’s **clearest board** is marked **MED–HIGH** (or higher) and the preferred entry **stays ≤ the plan band** through the ~1h lineup-confirm watch into KO (no thesis-killing XI/injury flip), **default to at least a probe fill** unless Ash explicitly says pass. Pure paper on that board is leave-on-table, not discipline. Live underwater marks after an early against-thesis goal are a sizing reason, not a skip reason. If the preferred print **never enters** the go band (stays rich), skip is correct — do not rewrite with FT.

**Why / source:** [2026-09-14 Villarreal–Betis](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-14-lal-vil-bet.md). **2026-09-20:** Villarreal vs Levante clearest (VIL No ≤36 / LEV ≤16 / Draw ≤20) **never filled** — prints over band; FT Villarreal **3–1** = luck dodge under paper/FT rule ([sunday paper](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-20-sunday-paper.md)).

## Live — don’t buy the dump as a “better print”

**Rule:** Do **not** treat a mid/late live dump of a morning favorite (e.g. **55¢ → mid-30s**) as an automatic better-print probe. Reassess *why* the board re-priced (Draw path, interim bounce, failed conversion) before buying the collapse. Rule 4’s probe-by-default is for **≤ plan band into KO**, not for chasing a post-dump live favorite on unfinished xG.

**Why / source:** [2026-09-15 Alavés–Valencia](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-15-lal-ala-val.md). **Held 2026-09-20:** Depor Yes dump after Cucho not re-bought; no Betis Yes morph ([dep-bet](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-20-lal-dep-bet.md)).

## Paper / non-fill — don’t rewrite with FT either way

**Rule:** Do **not** rewrite a paper sit or non-fill with FT. An unfilled **MED–HIGH clearest** probe that **loses** is still unfinished probe-by-default process unless Ash **explicitly passed** — FT luck ≠ a new sit preference. A deliberate **PASS outside the price band** that **wins** stays a correct band call — FT ≠ license to chase the rich print next time. Sister to “don’t rewrite a sell with FT.”

**Why / source:** [2026-09-16 RC Deportivo vs Sevilla](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-16-lal-dep-sev.md). **2026-09-20:** VIL No over-band skip → FT VIL 3–1 luck dodge; GET Yes PASS @46.5 → FT 1–0 still correct cap; ATM Yes ≤28 unfilled → FT 2–1 leave-on-table by band ([sunday paper](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-20-sunday-paper.md)).

## Entries — hard max price ≤45¢

**Rule:** **Never buy** a new soccer ticket above **45¢** (any Yes/No/Draw). Soft lean, chalk, or “clearest board” does **not** override. If the preferred band sits **>45¢**, **PASS** or wait for a print ≤45¢. Size/probing does not waive the cap. Standing auto-probe permission (Ash 2026-09-20) only fires when slate go ticket is still **≤ plan band and ≤45¢**.

**Why / source:** [2026-09-17 Málaga CF vs Villarreal CF](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-17-lal-mala-vil.md). **Held 2026-09-20:** three auto-probes all ≤29¢; LEE/MCI/MUN/GET/BET Yes all PASSed over cap.

## Instrument — prefer Draw when co-primary into grind

**Rule:** When the slate lists **Yes and Draw as co-primary** into a low-event / relegation-grind / “first win” script, prefer the **Draw** instrument for the auto-probe if both are in band — home Yes dies on a late equalizer Draw FT.

**Why / source:** [2026-09-20 RC Deportivo vs Real Betis](../journals/2026-09/2026-09-20-lal-dep-bet.md) — Depor Yes @25¢; Cucho 43' / Villares 86' → FT **1–1**; Yes −$5.19; Draw ≤26 would have paid.
